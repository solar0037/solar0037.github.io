**2024.05. ~ 2024.10. Rainfall Measuring Based on Data Subjects - Participation Prize**
- 2024 다학제 융합 IoT가전분야 캡스톤디자인 경진대회 [[GitHub](solar0037/rainfall-datasubject)]
- Tech: Arduino, Node.js
- Description: For my college IoT capstone contest, our team built a set of umbrellas that can measure rainfall in a small area. Since summer rainfall is concentrated on narrow zones, we thought providing users with realtime rainfall information could be useful. I developed a web interface that can recieve rainfall data from clients and display estimated rainfall in nearby areas via logarithmic interpolation. Throughout the whole contest, I learned that real-world applications are more than just technology itself, and defining the problem correctly is very important.

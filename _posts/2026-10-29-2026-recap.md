## 2026: A Break from Uni, but Staying Determined

This year (2026) I took a gap year from my university to study what I wanted to and experience lots of stuff from the world. It is still October, but I guess this is a good time to do a little recap.

### Studying

I reviewed what I have learned in class and even did some more. I read OSTEP (Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces): a book about operating systems fundamentals. I summarized Part 1 very fast, since I studied it in my OS class. After that, I continued to read Part 2 and 3. They were harder compared to Part 1, but I managed to understand them after all. I also did a summary on those chapters. After finishing this book, I felt proud of myself who stayed determined despite the difficulty.

### Mini-Lecture in Old High School

This was not a very complex task, but rather time-consuming. I had to prepare all the necessary items for each student attending my class. This includes a Raspberry Pi, so I had to install Raspberry Pi OS on each SD card and make sure it works well. I also had to run here and there to help students having problems connecting to their Pi. The biggest problem happened in the second part of the class: VNC's policies had changed and an account was required to use their viewer, which was not the case before. When we managed to see our Pi's screen, it was too late to test the image classifier model. Anyways, we successfully connected the camera to the Pi, so it was a half success and failure, I guess.

### Certificates / Languages

People say if you have certain certificates like 컴활 (컴퓨터활용능력, Computer Use Skills) or ADsP (Advanced Data Analytics Semi-Professional), you might get a slightly better chance on getting employed. So why not try? Both 컴활 and ADsP were pretty easy, and did not take a long time to acquire.

I also got my JLPT (Japanese) N2 certificate in January, so this year I am going for N1, which is the highest level on this test. After that, I can feel proud of myself that I have learned Japanese to an advaced level. Besides that, I am studying Chinese since I think it is more important in today's job market.

### Group Activities

I have been attending a language exchange club almost every week and conversed in English, Japanese and Chinese with foreign students (though it was mainly English). It has been a great opportunity to meet people from around the world and get to know each other.

### Volunteering

I recently joined an abandoned dogs care volunteering club and a stray cats TNR (Trap, Neuter, Release) volunteering club. I joined these groups because I love animals and want to understand what it is like to be responsible for a pet. I am still a beginner, but I will get better.
